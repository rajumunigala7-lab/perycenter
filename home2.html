About Aviora

A sanctuary built around curiosity, care and conservation.

Aviora is more than a destination. It is a place to slow down, observe closely and understand the remarkable world of birds. Every visit helps connect people with the ecosystems that sustain life.

  • Ethical bird care and protected habitats.
  • Educational programs for children and adults.
  • Guided experiences led by passionate naturalists.
  • Conservation programs supporting local ecosystems.

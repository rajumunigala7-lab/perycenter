The Falconry School

Where tradition meets responsible bird care.

Falconry is more than learning to fly a bird. It is a relationship built on observation, patience, trust and respect.

Our school introduces students to the principles behind responsible falconry while helping them understand the biology, behaviour and conservation of birds of prey.

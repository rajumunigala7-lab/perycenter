About Aviora

A sanctuary built around respect for the wild.

Aviora Falconry Bird Center brings people closer to the remarkable world of birds of prey through education, responsible falconry, conservation, and unforgettable experiences.

Our goal is simple: help people understand these extraordinary creatures and leave with a stronger desire to protect the natural world they call home.

  • Responsible and educational bird experiences
  • Hands-on learning about birds of prey
  • Wildlife awareness and conservation education
  • A safe and respectful environment for every bird

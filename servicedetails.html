Falconry Experience

Step into the world of falconry and discover the incredible relationship between people and birds of prey. Your experience combines close observation, expert guidance, responsible handling, and the unforgettable sight of a bird taking flight.

Your Experience

  • Welcome and introduction to the Aviora team.
  • Introduction to the birds participating in your session.
  • Learn about responsible falconry and bird welfare.
  • Guided bird interaction and observation.

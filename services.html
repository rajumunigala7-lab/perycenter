Featured Experience

Feel the power of a bird taking flight

There is something unforgettable about watching a bird launch into the air, circle overhead, and return with complete trust in its handler.

Our guided bird experiences are created around respectful interaction. You will learn about the bird's personality, behaviour, natural history, and the relationship between bird and falconer.

  • Small-group experiences
  • Experienced bird handlers
  • Educational interpretation
  • Bird wellbeing comes first
